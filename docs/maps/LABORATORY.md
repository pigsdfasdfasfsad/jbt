## Release identity

- **Release mode:** Regular, 15 waves
- **Environment / skybox:** Night
- **World location:** Alyth Corporation facility; region undocumented
- **Supported objective families:** Load, Repair, Radio, Unpack, Escort
- **Collectibles / easter-egg references:** Spiffo plush

## What the reconstruction must preserve

*Source: Laboratory.wiki*

- **In-game corporate slogan:** "Living life, living longer." — *Alyth Corporation*.
- **Layout character:** a large multi-level research building operated by *Alyth Corporation*, a
  scientific research and pharmaceutical company. **All players spawn at the front entrance reception desk.**
- **Landmarks:**
  - Two long, narrow **bridges** from the reception desk to a two-story main research facility.
  - West side, second floor: large offices, a meeting room, several smaller individual offices.
  - Below the second floor: a **construction site** of incomplete rooms including an accessible large
    office and another meeting room.
  - Opposite side: a spacious **parking garage spanning both floors**.
  - First floor: a **makeshift hospital ward** with tables, medical tents and emergency beds.
  - Under the main entrance: a long, **C-shaped cafeteria**.
  - North side: a large **server room** with a server office on the second floor, reached through a large
    research room, office and hallway; smaller research rooms behind the server office. First floor holds
    more large research rooms, a hallway to the parking garage, and a **long row of examination rooms**.
  - Also documented: break room, staircases, dead-end hallway and dead-end doors, broken vent above a staircase.
- **Infected spawns:** the parking lot next to the front entrance reception desk; **broken vents above
  inner staircases**; doors leading to dead ends on specific edges of the map; inaccessible floors in the
  parking garage; and **the broken roof above**.
- **Map-specific mechanics / details:**
  - Avoid short-ceiling areas when running solo — low ceilings let Bloater and Juggernaut Infected lob
    spore clusters directly at the player. The makeshift hospital ward is safer because its centre has
    **no ceiling to block lobbing angles** and it holds many item spawns.
  - The server room is reachable only by **two narrow paths** but has limited item spawns.
  - Parking-garage ramps gather infected into a tight group.
  - Laboratory's theme is heavily influenced by **The Lab** from *Escape from Tarkov*.
  - Two earlier versions of the map were scrapped; the third is the one in the game (see §9).
  - A former map GUI exists: `Vote-laboratory-NEW.png` (Note: Pre-February 20, 2022).

## Engine scaffold

Each map is reconstructed as five independently verifiable layers:

1. **Static art** — terrain, structures, props, decals, signs, vegetation and background dressing.
2. **Collision + navigation** — player collision, infected barriers, pathfinding modifiers, traversal blockers and drop-offs.
3. **Gameplay anchors** — player starts, infected spawns, item boxes, objective anchors, fortification positions and helicopter/supply-drop support points where applicable.
4. **Environment** — sky, fog/atmosphere, color grading, lights, local/environment sounds and weather presentation.
5. **Map metadata** — map name, voting card/icon, objective support, mode restrictions and reconstruction validation data.

## Build sequence

- [ ] Freeze/hash source map and associated evidence.
- [ ] Extract hierarchy, transforms, dimensions, materials, collision and named anchors.
- [ ] Generate a machine-readable instance inventory.
- [ ] Generate top-down bounds/outlines from extracted geometry.
- [ ] Build engine-neutral asset mapping and resolve external dependencies.
- [ ] Reconstruct static scene in Godot.
- [ ] Add collision/navigation and validate traversal.
- [ ] Add spawn, item and objective anchors.
- [ ] Recreate lighting, atmosphere and audio zones.
- [ ] Run visual/fidelity comparisons against supplied captures.
- [ ] Run gameplay acceptance tests and record known deviations.

## Completion evidence expected

A map is not considered reconstructed merely because it renders. Completion requires a source manifest, normalized scene data, top-down diagram, instance/asset inventory, collision/nav validation, spawn/objective validation, comparison captures, automated tests where possible, and a written deviation log.
